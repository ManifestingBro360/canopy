# Adopters

Companies, teams and products that use Canopy in real work are listed at
https://theprojectsierra.ai/adopters/.

To add yours, open an [adopter request](https://github.com/ManifestingBro360/canopy/issues/new?template=adopter.yml)
with a name, a link, one sentence on how you use it, and a logo you have the
right to share. Listing is free and means use, not sponsorship. To change or
remove an entry, open an issue.
